Home › Mission

Our Mission

Mission Statement

Over half a century ago, a young girl called Anayat Begum had a dream — a dream to empower young women through education. A vision beyond her time. Now, many years later, her children, with the support of those who share this vision, want to make this a reality with the implementation of Jamiah Anayah — a university of care.

JA provides a full English-medium schooling programme for Key Stages I to 5 (ages 4 to 18). Our educational programme is an all-through 14-year system allowing students to progress from Kindergarten at age 4 to Upper Secondary at 18.

The central aim of Jamiah Anayah is to give women dignity, confidence, and the power to change their lives. We want them to be beacons of light for future generations.

Serving

Providing the highest level of education for girls — ensuring proficiency in Urdu and English with the Pakistan Single National Curriculum.

Guiding

Preparing girls with the values and skills to become confident Muslims ready to make a positive contribution to community and society.

Inspiring

Providing young girls with tools to become self-sufficient leaders and adults, aspiring to affiliate with the Girls Guides programme.

Our Vision

Our Vision

To provide the highest levels of education for people in the local community — equipping young girls with the tools to contribute positively and to be self-sufficient as leaders and adults in the future.

We envision a school where Islamic values and international academic excellence go hand in hand — where every girl leaves with the confidence, knowledge, and qualifications to access universities worldwide and make a meaningful difference in the world.

Our curriculum, based on the internationally recognised Cambridge Assessment International Education framework, ensures a smooth and ambitious journey from Kindergarten through to A Level — offering qualifications welcomed by universities from the UK to Australia to the USA.

🎯 Academic Vision

Full Cambridge IGCSE affiliation with an all-through 14-year educational programme from Kindergarten (age 4) to Upper Secondary (age 18). Every student equipped to access global universities.

🌸 Social Vision

Young women who are confident, self-sufficient leaders — beacons of light for their families and communities, shaped by strong Islamic values and world-class education.

🏫 Institutional Vision

A fully-affiliated Cambridge school with Girls Guides membership, a robust community partnership with sponsors like Shiffa Trust, and a growing reputation for academic excellence in Pakistan.

🤝 Community Vision

Deep-rooted in the local community — accessible, welcoming, and committed to the long-term social and economic empowerment of girls and their families.

School History & Background

Our Story

From a Dream to Reality

The story of Jamiah Anayah International School begins over half a century ago, with a young girl named Anayat Begum. She dreamed of a world where young women could be empowered through education — a vision that was ahead of its time. Years later, her children, inspired by her vision, worked to bring it to reality through the establishment of Jamiah Anayah — a university of care.

50+
Over 50 Years Ago

The Dream of Anayat Begum

A young girl named Anayat Begum envisions empowering young women through education — a bold and forward-thinking idea in her era. Her dream plants the seed for what would become Jamiah Anayah.

▶
Pre-2023

Planning & Preparation

Her children, with the support of like-minded individuals, begin groundwork — designing the curriculum, preparing facilities, and assembling a team committed to the vision of a university of care.

🏫
12th March 2023

Inauguration of JA International School

Jamiah Anayah International School officially opens its doors with 3 students and 3 teachers (including the Acting Head). Kindergarten classes begin — the start of an educational journey with a strong mission.

📚
2023–2024

Growth & Development

The school expands rapidly. Staff attend Cambridge conferences to understand IGCSE affiliation requirements. Professional development sessions are held. Infrastructure is completed — labs, library, CCTV, solar power, and intercom installed.

🎓
2025 — Present

Expanding to Kindergarten – Grade 9

Classes now run from Kindergarten through Grade 9, with both Pakistan National Curriculum and IGCSE streams. The school continues to grow toward full Cambridge affiliation.

Rules & Regulations

At Jamiah Anayah International School, we believe that a clear and fair agreement between the school, parents, and students creates the best environment for learning.

🏫 As a School, We Commit To:

  • 1
    Provide a safe and happy environment where all children are valued, respected, and listened to.
  • 2
    Provide excellent teaching and an engaging curriculum to meet the needs of all children.
  • 3
    Provide as much support as possible to help each child meet their full potential.
  • 4
    Provide opportunities for children to practise at home what they have learnt in school.
  • 5
    Support each child in developing a moral and ethical compass through a Values-based Education philosophy.
  • 6
    Achieve high standards of behaviour by fostering positive social relationships, self-esteem, and a sense of responsibility.
  • 7
    Provide information to parents about school policies, meetings, and workshops.

👨‍👩‍👧 As a Parent / Guardian, We Expect You To:

  • 1
    Ensure your child attends school regularly and arrives on time wearing school uniform.
  • 2
    Inform the school of any concerns or worries that may be affecting your child's learning or behaviour.
  • 3
    Support our values-based education philosophy by encouraging a positive attitude toward every member of the community.
  • 4
    Attend meetings with your child's teacher and other staff in a positive and constructive manner.
  • 5
    Support and work with the school to uphold the school's behaviour policies.
  • 6
    Ensure the student has copies of the relevant workbooks and learning materials.
  • 7
    Ensure your child reads at least 2 books per week at home to develop proficiency in Urdu and English.

📘 Curriculum & Academic Policy

🇵🇰 Pakistan Single National Curriculum

All students follow the Government of Pakistan Single National Curriculum. Regular workbooks and home reading are required to support classroom learning.

🌍 Cambridge International (CAIE)

The school follows the Cambridge Assessment International Education curriculum — covering Early Years Foundation, IGCSE, AS, and A Levels — providing globally recognised qualifications.
